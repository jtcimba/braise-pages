Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Braise ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and display name when you create an account.
  • Usage data: grocery lists, recipe saves, and in-app interactions to improve the service.
  • Device information: device type and operating system for compatibility purposes.
  • Purchase information: subscription status managed through Apple App Store. We do not store payment details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve app functionality.
  • To sync your data across your devices.
  • To communicate important service updates.
  • To process subscription transactions.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with third-party service providers that help us operate the app (e.g., cloud storage, analytics), subject to confidentiality agreements.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see the Delete Account page.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Braise is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

Request Account Deletion

You can request the deletion of your Braise account and all associated data at any time. Once processed, this action is permanent and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All saved grocery lists and recipes
  • Your preferences and app settings

What is not deleted

  • Subscription records retained by Apple for billing purposes
  • Anonymized, aggregated usage data that cannot be linked back to you

How to request deletion

Send an email to jakecimbalista@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

Alternatively, use the Contact form and select "Account Deletion" as the subject.
